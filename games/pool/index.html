How to play · local two player

Take turns on the same device. Move the mouse to aim, or tap to set your aim on touch screens. Hold the table or the cue, drag backwards along the cue, then release. A longer pull makes a stronger shot. Lift without pulling to adjust your aim without shooting. Keyboard controls below the table offer fine aiming and a shot button. The first solid or striped ball potted assigns the groups. Pot your group, then the black 8 ball to win. Keep your turn after potting one of your balls without a foul.

Potting the white ball, hitting the wrong group first, or missing all balls gives the next player ball in hand: tap an empty part of the table to place the white ball, then aim. Potting the 8 ball early or with a foul loses the game. Simplified casual rules: no called pockets or cushion requirement. This is an original local game, with no online matchmaking.
