How to play · local two player

Take turns on the same device. Click or tap to aim the white cue ball, then choose a power and press Shoot. Use the arrow buttons for fine aiming. The first solid or striped ball potted assigns the groups. Pot your group, then the black 8 ball to win. Keep your turn after potting one of your balls without a foul.

Potting the white ball, hitting the wrong group first, or missing all balls gives the next player ball in hand: tap an empty part of the table to place the white ball, then aim. Potting the 8 ball early or with a foul loses the game. Simplified casual rules: no called pockets or cushion requirement. This is an original local game, with no online matchmaking.
